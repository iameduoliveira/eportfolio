Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this site.

What the site offers

  • Text size buttons (A− and A+) and a high-contrast mode at the top of every page. Your choice is remembered on this device.
  • A "Skip to main content" link, a logical heading order and visible focus outlines for keyboard users.
  • Every chart has a text readout or a table, and every interactive chart can also be used through ordinary buttons and lists.
  • Colour is never the only way information is shown: roles and award types also differ by pattern or shape.
  • Animations stop when your device is set to reduce motion.

Something not working?

Please email eduardo.oliveira@unimelb.edu.au and tell me what you were trying to do. I'll fix it as soon as I can.
